# Practice AI with combos (built Nov 23, 2026; numbers in AI Difficulty.md)

Chosen: one finale boss (Ascendant) after Lich; Adept to Lich slowed a few %; intro bots get 1-2 combos from their own elements.

## Who gets combos
Combos are top-of-ladder only. The early rungs stay as they are.

| Rung | Combos it casts | Counters your combos |
|---|---|---|
| Adept | Magma, Steam (rarely) | No |
| Magus | + Storm, Sunfire | Sometimes, slowly |
| Archmage | All but Glacier | Yes, and smashes your Glacier |
| Lich | All 9 | Yes, fast |
| **New final boss(es)** | All 9, full kit, used on purpose | Always tries |

New bosses always sit after Lich (see "Characters" below).

## When it picks each combo (fits the moment, like its Holy/Unholy picks)
- **Magma**: you're silenced or stunned and can't counter. Raw damage.
- **Storm**: you throw fast (light spam). The pulses punish throwing.
- **Advancing Glacier**: you're busy (drawing a signature, or silenced). It's a 5 s threat you must answer with C, F, I.
- **Twilight**: right before its own big spell, or when it's low on health.
- **Steam**: you've been landing a lot of hits in a row.
- **Thermal Shock**: you're already burning or chilled (sets them off).
- **Plasma**: you're drawing something long. The stun cancels it.
- **Sunfire**: it's hurt but not low enough to heal.
- **Grave**: you just healed or a regen is ticking.

## Countering your combos
- It reads your mix as you sign (C, then two letters) and answers with C + the two beating letters + circle.
- Your Glacier in flight: it always tries Fire + Ice (Lich almost always lands it).
- Combo counters take longer to sign (3 letters), so its reaction is slower than vs a plain charge. That's your window.

## Fairness
- It signs every letter like you do (C, then each element, each held), so its combos are visible and counterable.
- Same rules as the player (skills.md): no spamming one combo, never twice in a row.
