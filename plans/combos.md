# Combos (draft, numbers to tune)

C + two elements + a signature drawing unique to the combo. To counter one, you have to sign the counters to both elements.

| Combo | Effect |
|---|---|
| Magma (F+E) | Big hit plus a long burn. Re-signing doesn't clear it. |
| Storm (W+L) | 5 s of static, 0.5 s on / 0.5 s off. Each pulse does damage. Throwing during a pulse fizzles and does extra damage to the thrower. |
| Advancing Glacier (I+E) | Creeps toward the target for 5 s, then crushes them for heavy damage. Both sides keep fighting while it moves. It can be big, but the caster's other spells draw on top of it. It can only be stopped by a full counterspell (circle, no quick interrupt) with both counters: Fire for Ice and Ice for Earth. Its own spells and visuals never block that counterspell. Anti-spam: that counterspell also counters the caster if they're mixing or holding another Glacier. |
| Twilight (H+U) | About 5 s: you take 50% less damage and they take 50% more. Their part stacks with chill. |
| Steam (F+W) | About 4 s: their screen fogs and their attacks have about a 50% chance to miss. |
| Thermal Shock (F+I) | Works on its own (hit plus burn plus chill). If they already have burn or chill, it sets that off for bonus damage. |
| Plasma (L+F) | Stuns for about 1 s, Earthquake-style, and applies burn. No static. |
| Sunfire (H+F) | A long burn where each tick heals you. |
| Grave (U+E) | Blocks their healing for 9 s. Needs healing buffed first. |

## Drawings
Make drawings harder by adding parts, not by judging them strictly (strict curves aren't fun to draw).
- Magma: volcano closed with a base line (be a little strict). Storm: cloud and bolt (loose). Glacier: iceberg. Sunfire: sun. Grave: tombstone.
- Steam: closed puffy cloud with 3 humps on top, 1 on each side and 3 on the bottom. It must not be confused with Storm's cloud, which has a flat bottom and a bolt.
- Plasma: three tendrils, then a ball around them. Twilight: yin-yang plus both dots. Thermal Shock: thermometer with a zigzag crack.
