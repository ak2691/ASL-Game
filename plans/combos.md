# Combos (built Nov 23 2026)

Sign **C**, then two elements: the second one locks the mix. Then draw the combo's own drawing and push.
Counter one with **C + the two elements that beat its mix**, then a circle. Numbers live in `combat.ts`
(constants near the top); the Spellbook and Tutorial read the same ones.

| Combo | Mix | Drawing | Does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Magma | F + E | Volcano: slopes, a smoke loop, a base | A big hit, then a long burn |
| Storm | W + L | Cloud of exactly 4 bumps, a 3-line bolt below | Static pulses; a throw during one fizzles and costs damage |
| Glacier | I + E | Iceberg: waterline, peaks, the part below | Arrives slowly, can't be parried; only the Fire + Ice combo counter stops it |
| Twilight | A + U | Yin-yang with both dots | For a while you take less damage and they take more |
| Steam | F + W | Puffy cloud (3 humps on top, 1 each side, 3 below) | Their next attacks miss |
| Thermal Shock | F + I | Thermometer with a zigzag crack | A hit, a burn and a chill |
| Plasma | L + F | 3 loops from the middle, a line up, then the circle | Burns, knocks off their charge, drops their elements and parry to 50% power |
| Sunfire | A + F | A sun with exactly 8 points | A long burn that heals you what it deals |
| Grave | U + E | Tombstone with a cross | No healing or armor for them for a while |

Drawing rule: make a drawing harder by adding parts, not by judging it strictly.
